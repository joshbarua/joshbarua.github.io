Research interests

Nowadays, I mostly work on the interpretability of language models, with a focus on explanations rooted in data, learning, and representation. I'm particularly interested in questions and phenomena that have natural implications for our ability to control and align models. I often draw inspiration from more mature fields (e.g., cognitive science, linguistics, physics) that have made progress in understanding by developing simple, but principled, models of more complex systems.
